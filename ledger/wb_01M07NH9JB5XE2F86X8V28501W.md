Field design gap from PropertyCompass2 on alpha.5 (relayed from their mentorship-epic sibling session; near-miss, no data lost): `set.body` is a whole-body REPLACE, and a mirror consumer whose ledger item carries ledger-only content (their worked case: a "Title intent" annotation on wb_01M05EX0JT9M9CMBSVRX49HZ1A recording a divergence the mirror cannot follow because patch refuses title) can silently destroy it by regenerating the body from the upstream source. The destruction passes every check: inspect, current revision, well-formed body, CAS satisfied - state committed, ok true. The CAS fence is a byte-level lost-update guard with zero semantic safety, and the contract leaves the merge burden implicit ("replaces the whole body under create's body rules").

Consumer asks, cheapest first, with the orchestrator's dispositions:

1. DOCUMENTATION - SHIP REGARDLESS of the rest: mutation contract section 9 states plainly that set.body replaces and never merges, and that a consumer mirroring an external source MUST read-modify-write from the CURRENT item body, never regenerate from the source alone - regenerating discards every ledger-only byte the caller just read. The skill's body bullet gets the same sentence in agent voice. Docs guard both.

2. BODY-APPEND - decide with the machinery in front of you, bias YES if clean: a set.body_append member (string appended after the current body, same byte-splice serialization as #105's replace, mutually exclusive with set.body in one request) covers the dominant annotation shape without the consumer carrying a merge. If exclusivity or serialization complicates the request schema disproportionately, record why and skip it - the documentation is the load-bearing half.

3. body_must_contain - considered and DEFERRED, record the reasoning in the item's closing decision rather than the contract: it converts consumer discipline into a tool invariant, but it invites a false sense of semantic safety (contains-check passes while meaning is destroyed), grows the request schema for a speculative shape, and no consumer has yet lost data. Revisit on the first real loss.

Acceptance:
- Contract and skill state replace-not-merge and the read-modify-write rule; docs guards red when removed.
- If append ships: a fixture proves append preserves every existing body byte and the frontmatter, refuses when combined with set.body, and bumps updated; oracle mirrored both directions; version note appended to the v3 delta list with the request-schema-widening argument.
- The must_contain deferral and its reasoning recorded in the closing decision.
- Gate green on both runtimes.
