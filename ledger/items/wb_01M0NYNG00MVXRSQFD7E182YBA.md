Generate an original, whimsical Wowbagger brand image with Nano Banana for the GitHub page, npm package presentation, and Lee's LinkedIn profile. Use the Bowerick Wowbagger canon reference at https://hitchhikers.fandom.com/wiki/Bowerick_Wowbagger.

Creative direction:
- Evoke Bowerick Wowbagger the Infinitely Prolonged: tall alien silhouette, flattened head, slitty eyes, lustrous pale grey-green skin, thin spindly hands, and extravagantly draped golden robes with an unusual collar.
- Suggest his absurd cosmic mission and ennui with a playful spaceship, starfield, cosmic itinerary, or other light visual gag. Keep the mood warm, clever, and whimsical rather than menacing or crude.
- Create an original composition; do not copy an existing fan illustration, book cover, or recognizable artist's style. Do not place insults, quotations, or other text in the image.

Acceptance criteria:
- Produce a Nano Banana prompt and one approved master image.
- Master is square, 1024x1024 or smaller, with a strong silhouette and legibility at 128px.
- Export an optimized PNG or WebP small enough for GitHub, npm README assets, and LinkedIn upload; target <=500 KB without visible quality loss.
- Provide a 512px derivative or confirm the master scales cleanly to that size.
- Check square, circular, and narrow social crops; the character and core visual remain recognizable.
- Record the generation model, prompt, source reference, output dimensions, file size, and checksum.
- Confirm publication rights and attribution requirements before using the asset publicly.
