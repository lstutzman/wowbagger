## Problem

`claim-sync` returns only `{code: "conflicting-adoption", item_id}` when two adoption histories bind one source revision to different target revisions. It omits both sequence numbers, revisions, actors, and a safe remediation, so an operator cannot audit the conflict.

## Accepted evidence boundary

Property Compass item #1476 is accepted as-is. Its conflicting rows are seq 3309 (`71e8ced9` to `3f0f6415`, actor `omp-page-help`, commit `cf9feb692`) and seq 3477 (`3f0f6415` to `71e8ced9`, actor `omp-260817-214931`, commit `1615d5a32`). Current bytes later match revision `094c3fc3` at seq 4967. This item must not repair, reverse, or re-adopt those bytes or existing rows.

## Acceptance criteria

- `conflicting-adoption` identifies both conflicting journal entries with their sequence values, source and target revisions, and `adopted_by` values.
- Diagnostics name whether the conflict is local, incoming, or cross-history and give a safe human-audit remediation.
- The refusal remains non-mutating: no journal, reconcile log, item, or claim-store bytes change.
- Tests cover the accepted #1476 shape without changing its historical rows.
