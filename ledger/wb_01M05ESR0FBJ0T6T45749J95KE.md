Verified defect found during item #92 (envelope unification): the adapter maps a deterministic claim-fence refusal to `mutation-outcome-unknown`.

`spec/adapter-reference.js` `validCoreMutationEnvelope` and its mirror `src/adapter/process-outcome.js` require `value.command === command`, `contract_version === CORE_CONTRACT_VERSION`, exact root members, and no `namespace`. A real fence refusal (`namespace: ledger-mutation`, `command: create-v1`, `contract_version: 1`, exit 4 or 6) fails all three, so `core_envelope_valid` is false, `mutationAmbiguous` sets, and the adapter returns `mutation-outcome-unknown` with recovery guidance — even though the refusal says `state: "unchanged"` and provably wrote nothing. An agent driving the core through the adapter sees "the mutation may have been applied; inspect before retrying" on every fenced refusal on a provisioned ledger.

Reproduced directly against the oracle during #92 by feeding it a verbatim fence refusal at exit 4 (see #92's completion evidence). No test blesses the current behavior.

Scope:
1. Decide the shape the adapter forwards a claim-domain refusal in — this changes adapter outcome mapping, a distinct version domain with its own conformance vectors. Item #92's response-domain rule (mutation contract section 2) is the written prerequisite: the fence refusal is the work-claim contract answering, dispatchable by namespace before core-envelope validation.
2. Map fenced refusals to an honest deterministic outcome (state unchanged, the fence's own error detail forwarded), never to mutation-outcome-unknown.
3. Mirror in the oracle independently; conformance vectors for every fenced refusal class (claimed-item-write-refused, active-claim-write-refused, claim-store-unavailable); mutation-guard both directions.

Acceptance:
- A conformance vector proves a verbatim fence refusal maps to a deterministic unchanged outcome naming the claim-domain error, not mutation-outcome-unknown.
- Oracle and shipped adapter agree, mutation-guarded both directions.
- Four-command gate green on both runtimes.
