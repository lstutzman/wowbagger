Contract drift found during item #108: `inspect --number N` on a number no item carries emits `item-not-found` with `details: {number: N}`, but mutation contract section 5 and both adapter surfaces say item-not-found details contain only `id`. Pre-existing from #84. Unreachable through the adapter today (it always invokes --id), so nothing fails - but the emitted envelope and the contract disagree, which is exactly the class of drift #92 exists to prevent.

Scope: decide and align. Either the contract documents the number-selector variant (details carry `number` when the request selected by number - additive, version-note in the v3 delta list, pin with a fixture, mirror in the adapter surfaces only if they can ever see it), or the CLI resolves the selector first and emits the standard id-only shape with the number named in the message. Bias: document reality - the number is what the caller asked for, echoing it back is the honest detail.

Acceptance:
- Emitted envelope and contract agree; a fixture pins the number-selector not-found shape.
- envelope-domains manifest updated if the shape is a distinct response class.
- Gate green on both runtimes.
